I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Mathematics](https://liu.se/en/organisation/liu/mai) and am also affiliated with [WASP](https://wasp-sweden.org/) at Linköping University.

I was a postdoctoral associate in the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering](https://cse.umn.edu/cs) at University of Minnesota, working with [Professor Ju Sun](https://sunju.org/). I earned my PhD from the [Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://cse.umn.edu/isye) at University of Minnesota under the supervision of [Professor Zhaosong Lu](https://zhaosong-lu.github.io/). I received my bachelor's degree from the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://math.xmu.edu.cn/) at Xiamen University, with thesis advised by [Professor Wen Huang](https://www.math.fsu.edu/~whuang2/).

## Research Interests

I focus my research on data science and optimization. Recently, I have been exploring:

- Optimization methods/theories for deep learning
- Optimization methods adaptive to problem structure/geometry
